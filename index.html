Frequently Asked Questions

Can I sync my fitness device? ✖

Yes, you can sync your fitness device such as Apple Health (via iPhone) and Google Fit (via Android). We also support devices like FitBit, Apple Watch, and more. Syncing your device with Fit Rewards allows you to track your activity seamlessly.

Who can join the Fit Rewards program? ✖

Anyone can join Fit Rewards! Our program is open to fitness enthusiasts of all levels, whether you're just starting your fitness journey or are a seasoned athlete. Currently, Fit Rewards is available to users in New Zealand, with plans to expand globally in the future.

What are Fit Tokens and how do I earn them? ✖

Fit Tokens are a reward system designed to motivate your fitness journey. You earn them by completing activities tracked through your synced device, such as workouts, steps, or calories burned. For example, you can earn 50 Fit Tokens for a 30-minute workout (minimum 5000 steps) or 50 Fit Tokens for completing a 2-hour hike.

How much time do I need to commit? ✖

After registration, you can start earning Fit Tokens right away. There’s no minimum time commitment – you can engage as much or as little as you like. The more active you are, the more rewards you can earn. Fit Rewards is designed to fit into your schedule, whether you’re working out daily or just a few times a week.
